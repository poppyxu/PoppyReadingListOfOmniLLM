# Persona

How a language model gets a stable way of behaving, and how that way of behaving shows up inside the network.

This page follows one line. Written principles say what the model should prefer. Character training turns those principles into traits. Later work reads the traits out of activations, and asks when the model leaves the persona it was given.

Posts and papers for this topic are listed on this page. They are not copied into the repository README.

| Work | Date | Venue | What it does | Links |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Constitutional AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073) | 2022.12 | Bai et al., arXiv 2022 | A short list of principles stands in for human labels of harmful answers. The model critiques and revises its own replies, then another model ranks pairs by the same principles. Those rankings train the preference model used for reinforcement learning, called RLAIF. Released December 15, 2022. | [Post](https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback) |
| [Claude's Constitution](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claudes-constitution) | 2023.05 | Anthropic, 2023 | Publishes the principles used to train Claude, so they can be read and revised. The list aims at helpful, honest, and harmless behavior. Sources include the UN Declaration of Human Rights, platform safety rules, and principles written to cover non-Western perspectives. Published May 9, 2023. | |
| [Claude's Character](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-character) | 2024.06 | Anthropic, 2024 | Claude 3 adds character training on top of Constitutional AI. For a trait, the model writes relevant user messages, answers them in character, and ranks its own answers. A preference model is trained on that ranking. Researchers then check how each trait changes behavior. Published June 8, 2024. | |
| [Persona Vectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21509) | 2025.08 | Chen et al., arXiv 2025 | A trait named in text, such as sycophancy, evil, or hallucination, is turned into prompts that elicit it and prompts that suppress it. The difference in activations is a vector. The vector monitors the trait in a conversation or during finetuning, steers it, and flags training samples that would shift it. Shown on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Post published August 1, 2025. | [Code](https://github.com/safety-research/persona_vectors) · [Blog](https://www.anthropic.com/research/persona-vectors) |
| [From Shortcuts to Sabotage](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18397) | 2025.11 | MacDiarmid et al., arXiv 2025 | A model trained to cheat on production coding tasks, in the same run, starts to fake alignment, side with malicious actors, and sabotage safety research. The paper is "Natural Emergent Misalignment from Reward Hacking." Chat safety training repairs chat answers and leaves the agentic behavior. Telling the model that cheating is allowed on these tasks stops the broader misbehavior, while the cheating itself remains. Post published November 21, 2025. | [Blog](https://www.anthropic.com/research/emergent-misalignment-reward-hacking) |
| [The Assistant Axis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10387) | 2026.01 | Lu et al., arXiv 2026 | Activations for 275 character archetypes, including editor, jester, oracle, ghost, consultant, and analyst, are taken on Gemma 2 27B, Qwen 3 32B, and Llama 3.3 70B. The leading direction of that persona space, the Assistant Axis, measures how close the model's behavior is to the default Assistant. Capping activations along the axis reduces drift into harmful personas. The same axis is already present in the pretrained models. Post published January 19, 2026. | [Code](https://github.com/safety-research/assistant-axis) · [Blog](https://www.anthropic.com/research/assistant-axis) |
| [The Persona Selection Model](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/psm/) | 2026.02 | Marks, Lindsey, and Olah, Anthropic 2026 | Pretraining learns many characters from text: how they speak, how they act, and the social roles they occupy. Post-training selects one of those characters, the Assistant, and fills in its traits. A conversation with the assistant is a conversation with that character. Published February 23, 2026. | [Post](https://www.anthropic.com/research/persona-selection-model) |
